# SEO Strategy — Saudi Arabia Taxi Lead Generation Website

## Website Goal
Build a taxi/transfer lead generation website covering Saudi Arabia cities, airports, borders, hotels, religious destinations, tourist places, and intercity routes.

The website should generate leads through:
- WhatsApp inquiry buttons
- Quote request forms
- Call buttons
- Airport transfer pages
- City taxi pages
- Border transfer pages
- Long-distance route pages

## Primary SEO Focus
Main keyword theme:

**Taxi service in Saudi Arabia**

Supporting keyword themes:
- Saudi Arabia airport transfer
- Private taxi in Saudi Arabia
- Chauffeur service Saudi Arabia
- Intercity taxi Saudi Arabia
- Makkah taxi service
- Madinah taxi service
- Riyadh airport transfer
- Jeddah airport taxi
- Saudi border taxi transfer
- Umrah taxi service
- Hajj taxi service
- Hotel transfer Saudi Arabia

## Target Users
- Tourists visiting Saudi Arabia
- Umrah and Hajj passengers
- Business travelers
- Families needing private transfers
- Airport arrival passengers
- People traveling between cities
- People needing border pickup/drop-off
- Hotel guests

## SEO Structure
Use programmatic SEO pages for:
1. City pages
2. Airport pages
3. Route pages
4. Border transfer pages
5. Service pages
6. Tourist destination pages
7. Religious transfer pages

## Conversion Goal
Every page must include:
- WhatsApp CTA
- Call CTA
- Quote form
- Pickup location field
- Drop-off location field
- Date and time field
- Passenger count
- Luggage count
- Vehicle type
